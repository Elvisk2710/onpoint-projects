# Month 1 — complete modern African lifestyle poster pack

**Start with [all 30 posters](POSTERS.md).** The daily image files are in `images/lifestyle/`, named `day-01.png` to `day-30.png`. Open `index.html` locally for the visual gallery.

This is an undated 30-day sequence for the brand, using contemporary Zimbabwean homes, workplaces, commercial environments and infrastructure, with realistic illustrative African people.

- `captions.md`: matching channel-specific copy and alt text for all 30 days.
- `calendar.csv`: 30-day poster and caption index; map Day 1 to a Monday. Times are CAT.
- `posts.json`: structured copy, image paths and generation prompts.
- `month-01-content.pdf`: printable updated caption booklet.
- `images/lifestyle/day-NN.png`: full-resolution original generated poster.
- `images/lifestyle/day-NN.json`: poster headline, original topic and prompt provenance.
- `articles.md`: four weekly article drafts.
- `real-media-production.md`: optional authentic footage scripts.
- `experiments.csv` and `brand-guide.md`: supporting brand and measurement materials.

The posters are portrait approximately 4:5. Use fit-to-frame placement for Stories; separate 9:16 compositions are not part of this update. Earlier text-based feed/Story graphics, educational carousels and editable SVGs are retained as legacy educational materials; they are not editable source files for the new photographic posters. The previous single campaign concept is superseded by the daily collection.

All people and scenes in the new posters are illustrative generated models and environments. No social posts have been published or scheduled. Claims about real projects, staff or customer results still require genuine supporting media and approval.

# OnPoint Civils — real-media production and reel scripts

The finished graphic pack is usable without filming. The following optional scripts turn each day's topic into a 15–25 second reel when authorised real footage is available. They are production scripts, not finished videos. Read narration in a calm, natural voice; add captions and licensed music only if suitable.

Before filming: obtain permission for people and customer locations. Use genuine current footage for team, job and service claims. Do not generate customer evidence, staff identities or testimonials. For Civils, show PPE and approved activity; an engineer should review technical demonstrations. For Hygiene, follow product labels and technician instructions; never demonstrate chemical mixing. For ShieldTrac, remove addresses, faces without consent, number plates, codes, screens and exploitable system details; use a controlled staged demonstration where appropriate.

Each week: film a service overview, two process details, a wide contextual shot and a neutral closing shot. Capture vertical footage, steady frames and clear lighting. Do not interfere with work to get a shot. Replace unavailable authentic scenes with the supplied graphic; never present stock or AI imagery as a completed customer job.

## Publishing slots for filmed options

Civils: Friday video at 19:00 and Tuesday explainer at 19:00 on Shorts/TikTok, with Instagram/Facebook reuse. Hygiene: Monday, Wednesday, Saturday and alternate Tuesday at 19:30. ShieldTrac: Tuesday, Saturday and alternate Wednesday at 20:00. These replace the day's graphic when filmed; avoid duplicate feed posts with identical content. All times CAT.

## Evidence-dependent upgrades

- Project/job showcase: collect approved before/during/after media, dates, location at town level and verified scope. Use only confirmed quantities and outcomes.
- Team spotlight: collect the person's consent, name, actual role and approved quote. Do not infer training or experience from appearance.
- Testimonial: obtain the exact genuine quote, permission and the correct attribution. Use a real rating only if verified; no generated stars or reviews.
- Offer: confirm price, inclusions, availability and end date in writing. Current enquiry cards work without claiming free assessments, discounts or limited slots.
- Demonstration: label staged scenes. For security, do not show response routes, real operational screens or response-time promises.

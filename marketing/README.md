## Monthly production cycle

Month 1 is an undated 30-day pack. For subsequent months, create `month-02/`, `month-03/` and so on under the same brand folder. Review previous results; choose the next month's goals; collect genuine media and approved offers; write and design the new pack; review facts; assign actual dates; publish; record enquiries and bookings. No future packs or recurring automation are implied by this delivery.

## Weekly batching and measurement

Friday: collect authorised photos, site/job notes and client permissions. Sunday: prepare the following week's captions and artwork, check contact details and availability, and schedule the posts. Use unique campaign links where supported and ask new enquiries where they heard about the business. Record reach, saves, WhatsApp conversations, qualified enquiries, quotations and bookings. Compare like-for-like periods and treat low-volume results as directional. Test one variable at a time; do not invent results.

Image-generated illustrations are labelled conceptual; they do not document actual work. Finished templates give a publishable alternative while authentic job media is collected. Existing website image assets have not been assumed to be proof of a particular customer project.

## Part 1 — Marketing Strategy

### Positioning
**"Infrastructure built right, on time — from roads to reticulation."**
OnPoint Civils is the capable, reliable contractor: qualified engineers, its own machinery and a track record of finished projects.

### Target audiences
| Audience | What they worry about | What we tell them |
|---|---|---|
| Property developers | Servicing stands on time and on budget | Roads, water and sewer for residential and commercial developments |
| Councils, government, parastatals | Compliant, capable contractors | Registrations, track record, capacity, safety |
| Mines & agriculture | Remote access roads, water, structures | Mobilisation to remote sites with our own plant |
| Consulting engineers, QS, architects | Contractors who follow specs and deliver quality | Quality control, technical competence, communication |
| Schools, churches, private clients | Trustworthy builders | Design-and-build, transparent progress updates |

### Brand voice
Professional, technical but plain-spoken, confident. Let finished work and data (scope, dimensions, timelines) do the talking.

### Content pillars
1. **Project showcase (40%)** — progress updates, drone footage, before/during/after, completed projects
2. **Capability (25%)** — plant fleet, team, engineers, safety, quality control
3. **Expertise (20%)** — how things are built, costs, standards, lessons learned
4. **Company & calls to action (15%)** — milestones, hiring, "request a quote / send tender documents"

## Part 3 — Daily routine
- **On site every day:** the site supervisor takes 5 photos and 1 short clip (progress, machinery, team at work). Shoot the same spots each week for before/after comparisons.
- **Drone:** fly every active site every 2 weeks from the same positions
- **Permissions:** get client approval before naming a project; blur sensitive details where needed
- **Captions:** always include project type, location (town level), scope and a milestone
- **Engagement:** reply to LinkedIn comments the same day; connect with every person who engages
- **Friday:** collect the week's media into a shared folder and plan next week's posts

## Part 1 — Marketing Strategy

### Positioning
**"Clean, pest-free spaces — done properly the first time."**
OnPoint is the dependable, professional option for offices and homes that want visible results, safe chemicals and a team that shows up on time.

### Target audiences
| Audience | What they worry about | What we tell them |
|---|---|---|
| Offices, schools, clinics, restaurants | Health inspections, staff sick days, client impressions | Regular contract cleaning that keeps you inspection-ready |
| Homeowners & tenants | Cockroaches, bedbugs, rats, termites | Fast, safe fumigation that works the first time |
| Landlords, estate agents, Airbnb hosts | Move-in/move-out turnaround, tenant complaints | Same-week deep cleans and pest treatment between tenants |
| Warehouses, food processors | Pest audits, stock damage | Scheduled pest-control programmes with service certificates |

### Brand voice
Friendly, reassuring, practical. Short sentences. Show results; don't just describe them. Never use gross-out images without a solution next to them.

### Content pillars
1. **Proof (35%)** — before/after photos, transformation reels, job walk-throughs
2. **Education (30%)** — pest signs, hygiene tips, myth-busting, checklists
3. **Trust (20%)** — team, training, safety, chemicals used, testimonials
4. **Offers & calls to action (15%)** — bookings, free assessment, seasonal pushes

### Channels and their roles
| Channel | Role | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | Main reach for homeowners and SMEs; local groups | 1 post daily |
| Instagram (feed + reels) | Visual proof; reels for reach | 1 post daily + stories |
| WhatsApp Status & Business | Closest to the sale: offers, reminders, quick replies | 2–3 statuses daily |
| TikTok | Satisfying cleaning/transformation videos for reach | 3–4 per week |
| LinkedIn | Office managers, facility managers, HR | 2 per week |
| Google Business Profile | Local search ("fumigation near me") | 2 updates per week |
| Website blog | Search traffic and answers to common questions | 1 article per week |

### Best posting times (Zimbabwe time, CAT)

## Part 3 — Daily routine
- **Stories (IG/FB/WA):** 3–5 per day showing real jobs in progress, polls and questions
- **Engagement:** reply to every comment and DM within 1 hour during working hours
- **On every job:** take before/after photos from the same angle, plus 1 short video clip
- **Every completed job:** send the client a WhatsApp thank-you message with a Google review link
- **Sunday:** batch-create next week's graphics and captions

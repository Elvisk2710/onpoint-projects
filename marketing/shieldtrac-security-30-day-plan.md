## Part 1 — Marketing Strategy

### Positioning
**"Protection you can see and trust — 24/7."**
ShieldTrac is the security partner that is visible, accountable and responsive: vetted guards, modern technology and real reporting.

### Target audiences
| Audience | What they worry about | What we tell them |
|---|---|---|
| Homeowners (suburbs) | Break-ins, slow police response, family safety | Alarm + rapid response, CCTV you can watch on your phone |
| Shops, SMEs, fuel stations | Theft, staff shrinkage, robberies | CCTV + guards that prevent losses |
| Corporates, NGOs, schools, estates | Reliable guarding, compliance, accountability | Vetted, trained guards with supervisor patrols and reports |
| Construction sites, warehouses | Night theft of materials and equipment | Night guards, patrols and temporary CCTV |

### Brand voice
Confident, calm, professional. Reassure people; don't scare them. Talk about prevention and peace of mind more than about crime.

### Content pillars
1. **Safety education (35%)** — home/business security tips, checklists, "spot the weakness"
2. **Capability & technology (25%)** — CCTV installs, alarms, control room, response vehicles
3. **People & trust (25%)** — guard training, vetting, team spotlights, testimonials
4. **Offers & calls to action (15%)** — free assessments, packages, festive campaign

## Part 3 — Daily routine
- **Stories:** 3–5 per day showing installs in progress, guard posts (without exposing client locations), polls ("Do you have an alarm?")
- **Privacy rule:** never show client addresses, house numbers, gate codes or camera positions that could be exploited. Blur faces and number plates.
- **Engagement:** reply to every comment and DM within 1 hour; move pricing questions to WhatsApp
- **On every install:** take before/after photos and a short clip, with client permission
- **Monthly:** send each client a short service report (shows value, gives content ideas)
